I've been wondering why I find it difficult to find a sufficient quantity of birds to take photos of, when I try. Sometimes I find loads, but not "on-demand". So, I was looking into it, and it turns out that I should be going out at dawn to catch them when they're waking up and feeding. It seems like maybe this is something I should've already known.

![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0472.jpg "Tall trees at sunrise"){: .right}

In addition to this, I found an amazing website -- [artportalen.se](https://artportalen.se/) -- which has reports from all over Sweden when people see birds. Not only birds, in fact, also mushrooms? And some mammals. So I was looking around my area, and it seemed like Lerum would be a nice place to go for some photography. I found a decent looking woodland area on Google maps' satellite view, which I thought would have lots of open areas too (therefore easier to see birds, and more light).

I then, this morning, woke up at 5, and took the train (which was actually a bus) to Lerum! In the end I didn't photograph _too_ many birds, but got some really atmospheric autumnal landscape shots instead. And I _saw_ lots of birds! The most interesting of those that I saw or heard were a buzzard, several ravens, and I even definitely heard a heron, but didn't see it! There were also, as always everywhere, quite a few pigeons flying overhead (pictured left).

## Fog and Clouds

I arrived at the forest just before 7am, at which point the sun hadn't even begun to rise. I kept wondering whether bears exist this far down in Sweden...

![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0469.jpg "The fog was really low down, and little particles of water kept getting on my lens!")
![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0511.jpg "Classic autumnal colours")

Last night the forecast predicted clear skies, which... Well, when I arrived in Lerum I could see stars, but strangely the fog seemed to lift from the ground until it filled the sky. After a little while, a bit of blue did appear. Only for a moment, though.

![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0568.jpg "This is basically the only time the sky was at all clear.")
![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0569-1.jpg "The mist in the distance was so thick, it almost looked like a fire.")

## Lerum

In addition to the forest being nice, Lerum in general seemed pleasant, though I didn't spend much time aside from walking between the woods and the station.

![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0625.jpg "Stereotypically Swedish-looking red hut by a big lake.")
![](https://nc.jgarby.uk/public/p/2026/oct/_MG_0621.jpg "Watery plants")

I'll definitely go back at some point. The fog ended up giving the photos some nice atmosphere, but next time I'm hoping for a clear and bright morning.
